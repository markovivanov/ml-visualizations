About the data

Examples come from the existing Denver classroom CSV, based on Inside Airbnb’s June 30, 2026 release. The tree, forest, and PCA use the same 1,432 training listings as the class slides. The forest animation uses three shallow trees and two inputs for visibility; the notebook’s full forest uses 200 trees and three inputs. Prices are listed nightly USD. The six-point regression is the separate teaching close-up from the notebook. Data: Inside Airbnb, CC BY 4.0. Everything in this lab runs locally in your browser.
